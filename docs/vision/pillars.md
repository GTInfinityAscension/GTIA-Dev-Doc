# Design pillars

GTIA is GregTech about **building and running megafactories**, on a Kardashev-scale arc: from the first steam machine to planetary, stellar and galactic engineering. It sits between GTNH, GregTech Odyssey and GregTech Leisure: GTNH's power options and detailed tooltips, GTO's fast early game and humour, GTL's clean pack without magic, and none of the free endgame items of GTL's modified editions.

## The ten pillars (D-333)

1. **Kardashev scale.** Eras are named after it; the endgame has Mega and Hyper blocks, Dyson spheres and Matrioshka brains.
2. **Survival is never a requirement.** No dungeons, mob farming, food systems or exploration gates. Vanilla hunger.
3. **Mining without punishment.** Good tools early, with Fortune that counts on GregTech ores; later the Entangled Miner.
4. **Easy building.** FTB Ultimine from the start, the personal dimension in Steam, building gadgets and a multiblock builder.
5. **Early AE2.** Storage and first automation in MV, refinement in HV.
6. **Space with purpose.** Each destination has resources that make sense; visit once, then automate it by data.
7. **Many ways to make power,** GTNH-style: steam turbines, combustion, semi-fluid, gas, then fission and nitrobenzene in EV.
8. **Wireless energy in HV–EV.**
9. **Detailed multiblock tooltips** like GTNH's, with a GTO-style joke line.
10. **Satisfactory feeling.** Planning ratios and managing big factories, almost no survival.

## Working rules

- **Grind is solved with more factory, never with more clicking.**
- **Real chemistry, no PhD required,** as recipes in GTCEu and GTNA machines. A custom multiblock is added only when no existing machine can do the job (D-335).
- **Nothing skips a tier.** Shortcuts are same-tier kits and a vending machine, never free endgame items.
- **Every quest is reachable.** A checker reads the pack's real recipes and fails the build if a quest asks for something you cannot make yet.

## What GTIA does not do

- No magic mods.
- No research tree, research points or production quotas as gates.
- No ULV era. The campaign goes Start → Steam → LV → MV → HV → …
- Aesthetics never count toward progress.
