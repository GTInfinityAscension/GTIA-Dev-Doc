# Tiers

The full roadmap exists as a guide; a tier is only polished after the previous one has been played. Each chapter is drawn as a flowchart and approved before any quest is written.

| Tier | Line length | Signature content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Start** | — | First tools and the way into Steam |
| **Steam** | 2–3 steps | Wood and coal chemistry (Wood Retort, Still), lime kiln and fluxed steel, the GTNA large steam machines, first circuit by hand |
| **LV** | 4–6 | Chlor-alkali (Diaphragm Cell), real steelmaking, wood line to plastic, EBF and Aluminium. AE2 storage at the end of LV |
| **MV** | 4–6 | Gems and laser engraving, silicon and chips, stainless steel and Kanthal; Bayer Digester, Hall-Héroult Cell, Contact Converter, Ammonia Converter, Solvay Tower. AE2 autocrafting. Greenhouse and Pyrolyse Oven. Agronomica metal crops |
| **HV** | 6–10 | Full refining (Claus and more), the Moon, bees (Industrial Apiary) |
| **EV** | 6–10 | Mars and Venus/Mercury, electric Void Miner per planet, discovered fluids |
| **IV+** | 10+ | Zero-gravity production, Draconium, improved chemistry (rotary kiln, membrane cell, Hall-Héroult cell lines, double absorption) |

Gases come from planets: CO₂ on Mars, H₂SO₄ on Venus. Rare elements only appear as by-products of specific lines.
